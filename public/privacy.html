Privacy

What we keep, and why.

Fairylearn keeps as little as it can. Here is exactly what is stored, where it lives and who sees it.

Last updated October 28, 2026

The short version

  • You can try the free lessons and quizzes without an account. That progress stays in your browser.
  • With an account we store your email, your name and your learning answers, so your path is saved.
  • Your answers and questions are sent to OpenAI to build your path, write lessons and power your guide.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.

01Using Fairylearn without an account

You can read the three free lessons, take their quizzes and preview a learning path without signing up. Everything you do there is saved only in your browser’s local storage on this device: lesson progress, best quiz scores, trophies and items, your study journal, your theme, and your preview answers. We never receive it.

Clearing your browser’s site data for Fairylearn removes all of it.

02What we store when you create an account

Accounts are handled by Supabase Auth. When you sign up, Supabase stores:

  • your email address
  • your password, which Supabase keeps in hashed form (we never see it)
  • the name you tell us to call you

After you answer the five questions, we save one learning profile for you: your answers, your starting level, a short description of your path and the lessons in it, and when it was last updated.

The database checks every request, so a learner can only ever read or change their own profile.

03How AI is used

When AI features are switched on, our server sends some information to OpenAI:

  • your five answers and the lesson catalog, to choose your starting level and lessons
  • your answers and a lesson topic, to write a personal lesson
  • your recent guide conversation (up to ten short messages), your answers, your level and the current lesson title, to reply as your guide

Requests ask OpenAI not to store the conversation. Your email and password are never sent to OpenAI.

Your guide conversation is not saved by Fairylearn. It lives in the open page and disappears when you leave. Generated lessons are kept in the server’s memory for up to 24 hours so reopening one is instant, and are cleared when the server restarts.

04What stays on your device

Some things are always kept in your browser, even with an account:

  • your sign-in session, so you stay logged in across tabs
  • lesson progress, quiz scores, trophies and your study journal
  • your theme and this storage notice choice

Logging out removes your sign-in session from this browser. Progress saved in the browser stays until you clear the site’s data.

05Cookies, ads and tracking

Fairylearn does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking cookies and does not sell or share your information. We use your browser’s local storage only for the things listed above.

06Safety limits

To keep the service fair and protect against abuse, the server counts requests per network address and per account for a short time, for example how many lessons or guide messages you asked for this hour. These counts are kept in memory and are not linked to anything else.

07Students and age

Fairylearn is made for high school students, grades 9 to 12. If you are under 13, please don’t create an account on your own. Ask a parent or guardian first; you can still explore the free lessons without one.

08Deleting your data

To remove everything kept on your device, clear the site data for Fairylearn in your browser settings.

To delete your account, contact the site owner. When an account is deleted, its learning profile is deleted with it automatically.

09Changes to this policy

If the way Fairylearn handles information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

This is a plain-language policy written for students and families. It is not legal advice.
