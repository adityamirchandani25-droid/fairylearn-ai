How placement works

Five quick questions, about two minutes. There are no wrong answers, only a starting point.

  1. 01

    How well do you know AI?

    There’s no wrong place to start. We’ll meet you where you are.

  2. 02

    A little knowledge check

    What does a generative AI model do? It’s okay if you’re unsure.

  3. 03

    What brings you here?

    Pick the goal that feels most like you. You can always explore more later.

  4. 04

    How do you like to learn?

    Let’s make the journey feel natural to you.

  5. 05

    Make a little room for wonder.

    How much time would you like to spend learning each day?

Question 1 of 5About 2 min

How well do you know AI?

  • A fresh startI’ve heard about AI, but haven’t really tried it.
  • A little curiousI’ve tried ChatGPT or another AI tool a few times.
  • Getting comfortableI use AI regularly and know the basics.
  • Ready to go deeperI build with AI or understand how models work.

How your level is chosen

Your answer to question 1 sets a base level: A fresh start → Starter, A little curious → Beginner, Getting comfortable → Medium, Ready to go deeper → Expert.

If the knowledge check isn’t “Creates from learned patterns”, you start one level lower, but never below Starter.

With a free account, AI can fine-tune that level and order 10 to 14 approved topics for you.
