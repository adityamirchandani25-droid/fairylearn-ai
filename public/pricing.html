Questions, answered

Still wondering? Peek at a lesson first. It’s the quickest way to see if Fairylearn feels like you.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial and nothing to unlock later. Lessons, quizzes, your path and the guide are all free.

Do I need an account?

Not to start. The three essentials, quizzes and a preview path all work without one. Make a free account when you want your path saved and a guide to talk to.

What does the AI do with my answers?

Your five answers help it choose your starting level and the order of your lessons. Guide chats stay in your browser’s memory, are cleared when you log out, and are sent with storage turned off.

Can my school use it?

Anyone can use Fairylearn, in class or at home. There isn’t a special school plan yet, so each student makes their own free account.

What happens to my data?

Your answers and path are saved to your account so they follow you. Progress, trophies and settings stay in your browser. Read the privacy page for the details.
